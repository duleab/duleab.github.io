Workforce & Talent Strategy

This HR dashboard provides strategic insights into workforce management, measuring the effectiveness of talent acquisition pipelines against attrition rates, and evaluating human capital investments.

Key Finding: Maintained a positive net workforce growth (34.39K net additions) while achieving optimal training budget utilization, highlighting strong talent retention in a high-turnover industry.

Metrics Breakdown

What We Tracked

  • Workforce Analytics: Real-time headcount tracking, year-over-year expansion, department distribution, and regional density mapping.
  • Talent Acquisition: Hiring pipelines, time-to-fill efficiency metrics, sourcing channel effectiveness, and onboarding success tracking.
  • Retention & Turnover: Tracking of 156K departures against 190K hires, exit interview analysis, and employee satisfaction scorings.
  • Training ROI: Budget distribution mapping, skill program completions, and leadership succession planning.
