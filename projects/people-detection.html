Project Overview

Robust Crowd Analytics

This project implements a scalable people counting pipeline. The detector produces high-confidence bounding boxes per frame, while a lightweight tracking approach (SORT/DeepSORT-style logic) ensures that unique identities remain stable across frames, even through transient occlusions.

Technical Focus: Optimised for edge-device deployment by balancing deep learning accuracy with real-time OpenCV pre/post-processing efficiency.
