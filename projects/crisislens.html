The Problem

Disaster Information is Scattered, Generic, and Slow

During disasters, critical information is spread across multiple government portals (BMKG, BNPB), news sites, and social media — making it nearly impossible to act quickly. Worse, every platform gives the same generic alerts regardless of who is reading them. A rescue team needs different information than a tourist on a beach, yet they both receive the same notification.

Key Insight: The problem isn't a lack of disaster data — it's the absence of a system that turns that data into role-specific, instantly actionable decisions. This gap costs lives.

The Solution

One Disaster. Seven Intelligent Responses.

CrisisLens ingests real-time data from 12 trusted sources, processes it through a Gemini AI reasoning engine, and delivers personalized guidance through seven role-specific AI Copilots — all through a single unified platform.

AI Pipeline

From Raw Data to Life-Saving Decisions

  1. 1

    Collect

    Live ingestion every minute from 12 sources: USGS, BMKG, GDACS, NASA EONET, WHO, UN News, ReliefWeb, OCHA, FloodList, GDELT, Google News, and PreventionWeb.

  2. 2

    Verify

    AI-powered source credibility checking, cross-reference validation, duplicate detection via hash-based deduplication, and confidence score assignment.

  3. 3

    Analyze

    Gemini Flash classifies crisis type, severity, geographic impact, and population exposure using structured JSON extraction.

  4. 4

    Reason

    Gemini Pro generates natural language situation summaries, risk analysis, impact predictions, and explainable decision support with source citations.

  5. 5

    Personalize & Deliver

    Role-based filtering routes the right intelligence to the right Copilot — in real-time, with full Arabic/Indonesian/English language support.

Technology Stack

Built for Scale and Reliability

A fully containerized, production-ready architecture built in 48–72 hours for the hackathon, yet designed to scale to ASEAN-wide deployment.

  • Frontend: React 18 + TypeScript + Vite, Leaflet.js interactive maps, Vanilla CSS
  • Backend: FastAPI (Python 3.12), async SQLAlchemy, APScheduler for auto-ingestion
  • AI / LLM: Gemini Flash (fast classification) + Gemini Pro (deep reasoning & chat copilots)
  • Database: PostgreSQL 16 with 2,000+ real crisis events; hash-based deduplication
  • Infrastructure: Docker Compose (3-container stack), GitHub Actions CI/CD for daily data collection
