Business Context

Benchmarking Mobile Banking

This project compared customer feedback for three Ethiopian mobile banking apps (CBE, BOA, Dashen) using publicly available Google Play reviews. The goal was to turn unstructured feedback into a clear, prioritized list of product issues and opportunities.

Intelligent Pipeline: Created a high-performance scraping pipeline using ThreadPoolExecutor, feeding into a DuckDB OLAP engine for fast querying and advanced SQL Window Functions for sentiment trend tracking.

NLP Architecture

Theme & Sentiment Extraction

Short app-store reviews tend to be informal, noisy, and contain slang or typos. To counter this, I utilized RoBERTa-Twitter for sentiment classification, which natively understands social-media style text.

For topic clustering, I utilized BERTopic combined with HDBSCAN to surface recurring issues like crashes, login/OTP friction, and transaction failures without having to hand-label thousands of reviews.

Key Findings

  • BOA Structural Failure: 23 months of consecutive majority-negative sentiment.
  • CBE Perception Gap: Negative reviews are 51pp more "helpful" than positive ones.
  • Dashen Decay: Post-launch sentiment dropped 44pp over 12 months.
  • Engagement: Near-zero developer reply rates across all banks.
