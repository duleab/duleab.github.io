Wildfire Early Warning System

An AI-powered system predicting 72-hour fire risks for vulnerable indigenous communities in Borneo & Sumatra, integrating NASA satellite detections, meteorological data, and XGBoost modeling with automated bilingual alerts.

Data Engineering

Integrating Global Satellite and Meteorological Data

The system integrates real-time active fire detections from NASA's VIIRS and MODIS satellites, combined with 35-year CHIRPS rainfall history to compute localized SPI drought indices. These environmental factors are essential in identifying escalating fire risks before ignition occurs.

Key Insight: Merging the MODIS NDVI vegetation health index with ERA5 wind models allowed the system to construct "upwind neighbor fire exposure" — a critical compound risk metric that significantly increased early detection recall.

Early Warning Operations

Automated Bilingual Alerting

The goal of the system is operational readiness for indigenous communities in Borneo and Sumatra. Predictions from the XGBoost model are translated into automated, bilingual (English + Bahasa Indonesia) SMS-ready alerts, dispatched a full 72 hours before estimated critical fire exposure.

Pipeline Architecture

Four-Stage ML Pipeline

  1. 1

    Multimodal Data Ingestion

    NASA FIRMS API, Copernicus ERA5 wind data, and CHIRPS rainfall, aggregated to GADM district level for spatial consistency.

  2. 2

    XGBoost & Optuna Optimization

    Trained an XGBoost Classifier using strict walk-forward temporal cross-validation to prevent data leakage. Optimized over 50 Optuna trials targeting the F2-score to prioritize recall over precision.

  3. 3

    SHAP Per-District Explainability

    SHAP TreeExplainer breaks down why each specific district is flagged for risk, generating actionable transparency for disaster responders and community leaders.

  4. 4

    Interactive Folium Dashboard

    Predictions rendered on a live, interactive choropleth map using Folium, displaying 72-hour fire spread projections overlaid with population density grids.
