Customer Experience Insights

This interactive dashboard provides deep insights into customer behavior, satisfaction levels, and network performance across Alfamart's retail ecosystem.

Key Finding: Strong customer base growth with 246.6K active members demonstrating market trust, alongside consistent daily engagement showing sustained customer loyalty.

Metrics Breakdown

What We Tracked

  • Customer Base Analytics: Real-time monitoring of registered customers, membership growth trends, and segmentation for targeted marketing.
  • Traffic & Engagement: Average daily visits with peak hour analysis, store performance across the 44-store network, and engagement heatmaps.
  • Feedback & Satisfaction: Comprehensive tracking of 39K customer complaints with resolution timelines, satisfaction surveys, and service quality metrics.
  • Network Optimization: Strategic placement analysis, market coverage, and data-driven insights for future store expansions.
