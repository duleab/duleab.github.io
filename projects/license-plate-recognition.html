The ALPR Mission

Scalable Vehicle Identification

Scalable vehicle identification is crucial for modern traffic management, tolling, and security. Standard OCR struggles with the motion blur, varied lighting, and angled perspectives common in traffic footage. This project solves these challenges through a robust 4-stage pipeline that handles everything from initial vehicle detection to the deduplication of noisy video OCR reads.

Methodology

4-Stage Processing Pipeline

  1. 1

    Heavyweight Detection (YOLO)

    Trained a custom YOLO model on 27,900 images. Achieved a 0.9195 mAP@0.5 on the test set, outperforming the validation split and indicating excellent generalisation across different camera angles and lighting.

  2. 2

    Crop & Padding

    Bounding boxes are cropped with a precise 5% fixed padding to ensure characters near the edge of the plate are not cut off before OCR.

  3. 3

    Advanced Image Preprocessing

    Applied CLAHE (Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization) and Bilateral filtering. This enhances character edges and reduces noise, leading to significantly more reliable OCR reads in low-light conditions.

  4. 4

    OCR & Deduplication

    EasyOCR extracts the text. To handle flickering frame-by-frame reads in video streams, a Levenshtein distance algorithm (0.72 similarity threshold) is used to cluster and deduplicate noisy OCR reads into a single, confident final string.

Full-Stack Deployment: Architected a Streamlit web application supporting batch image processing and frame-by-frame video analysis. Models are cached per session for instantaneous interactive performance.
