Humanitarian Mission

Anticipatory Intelligence for Humanitarian Organizations

Missing a food crisis costs lives. This system integrates 207,682 armed conflict events (ACLED), monthly precipitation anomalies, and historical IPC Phase data to help humanitarian organizations pre-position aid 3 months before a crisis peaks. It targets the prediction of IPC Phase 3+ (Crisis) and above — the critical threshold for emergency intervention.

Methodology

Three-Stage Pipeline

  1. 1

    Multi-Source Data Fusion

    Engineered a pipeline to ingest and synchronize 207,682 conflict events, monthly precipitation anomalies, and historical IPC Phase data. Designed a 32-feature dataset capturing conflict intensity, rainfall shocks, and seasonal hunger patterns.

  2. 2

    XGBoost Modeling & Optimization

    Deployed XGBoost with hyperparameter tuning via Optuna and walk-forward temporal cross-validation. Implemented a dual-threshold strategy: F1-Optimal for standard operations and Humanitarian-Mode (Threshold=0.07) for maximum crisis sensitivity.

  3. 3

    SHAP Explainability for Decision Makers

    SHAP waterfall plots explain the specific regional risk drivers for every prediction — e.g., Boko Haram activity + Lean Season peak — transforming black-box predictions into actionable forensic intelligence for aid workers.

Design Philosophy: Achieved a 1.6% False Negative Rate using the Humanitarian-Mode threshold — ensuring that only the most isolated "black swan" events bypass the warning system, prioritizing no missed crises over false alarm reduction.
