Project Overview

Targeted Risk Segmentation

Insurance profitability hinges on accurately assessing risk. This project leverages historical claims data to segment customers, specifically identifying "low-risk" profiles that can be offered premium reductions without negatively impacting the portfolio's overall loss ratio.

Key Findings

Identifying Low-Risk Drivers

Rigorous statistical analysis and hypothesis testing across geographic, demographic, and vehicle characteristic dimensions revealed clear low-risk segments:

  1. 1

    Geographic Clusters

    Drivers in the Western Cape and Gauteng provinces showed significantly lower claim frequencies.

  2. 2

    Demographics

    Statistically, male drivers in the dataset exhibited a lower risk profile.

  3. 3

    Vehicle Characteristics

    Hatchback and Sedan vehicle types, specifically from Toyota and Volkswagen, correlated with lower average claims.

Portfolio Health: The identified low-risk segment demonstrated an excellent 9.2% loss ratio and a 14.5% claim frequency, strongly validating the segmentation strategy.

Data Pipeline

Reproducible MLOps with DVC

To ensure reproducibility and maintainability, the entire data science workflow was orchestrated using Data Version Control (DVC).

  • Data Preparation: Automated cleaning, missing value imputation, and outlier handling.
  • Feature Engineering: Systematic creation of risk ratios, temporal features, and interaction terms.
  • Model Training: Version-controlled training of Random Forest and Logistic Regression baselines for transparent risk assessment.
