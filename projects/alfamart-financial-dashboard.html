Dashboard Analytics

Financial Health & Trajectory

This dashboard acts as a command center for financial leadership, tracking fiscal performance over a 4-year period. It uncovers macro trends while permitting deep dives into specific operating segments.

Key Finding: Strong revenue growth trajectory with consistent month-over-month improvements alongside an optimized cost structure yielding higher net margins.

Metrics Breakdown

What We Tracked

  • Revenue Performance: Monthly trends showing seasonal variations, year-over-year comparisons, and granular breakdown by product category.
  • Profitability: Real-time tracking of gross profit margins, operating expense ratios, and net profit trends.
  • Cash Flow Management: Tracking operating, investing, and financing cash flows to ensure liquidity.
  • Financial Health: Liquidity ratios (current, quick), leverage metrics (debt-to-equity), and efficiency ratios (asset turnover).
