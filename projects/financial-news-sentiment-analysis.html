Objective

Predicting Market Movement Through NLP

Financial markets are highly reactive to news. This project develops an intelligent system that predicts stock market movements by analyzing the sentiment of financial news headlines. By quantifying sentiment, the model provides an objective, data-driven market signal.

Multi-Model Approach

Comparing NLP Architectures

To find the optimal balance between inference speed and contextual understanding, three distinct neural network architectures were implemented and compared:

  1. 1

    CNN (Convolutional Neural Network)

    Treated text as a 1D spatial sequence, capturing local n-gram patterns and short-term semantic relationships.

  2. 2

    LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory)

    Modeled the sequential nature of language, successfully capturing long-range dependencies within longer headlines.

  3. 3

    DistilBERT (Transformer)

    Leveraged pre-trained attention mechanisms to deeply understand financial context, achieving the highest performance at 94.2% accuracy.

Key Result: The system successfully established a statistically significant correlation (0.73) between extracted news sentiment and actual historical stock price movements.

Technical Integration

Correlating Sentiment with Technical Indicators

Sentiment alone is not a trading strategy. The pipeline integrates the NLP sentiment scores with traditional quantitative technical indicators—including RSI (Relative Strength Index), MACD, and Bollinger Bands—to create a holistic, multi-factor market analysis dashboard.
