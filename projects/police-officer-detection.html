Project Overview

Specialized Uniform Recognition

Standard pedestrian detection models treat all people identically. This project focuses on fine-grained visual classification, training a YOLOv8 model to specifically isolate and identify law enforcement personnel in diverse, crowded environments by analyzing distinct uniform features, tactical vests, and badges.

Security Integration: Capable of analyzing CCTV streams to track police presence at large events or verify law enforcement response times in emergency situations.

Output Results

Detection Robustness

The model performs exceptionally well in complex environments, accurately filtering out civilians while maintaining bounding boxes on officers.
